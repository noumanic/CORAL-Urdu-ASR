   - If multiple models produce the same word with reasonable confidence (>0.60), trust it
   - Higher agreement = higher likelihood of correctness

2. **Confidence-Based Selection**
   - High confidence (>0.80): Strong signal, likely correct
   - Medium confidence (0.50-0.80): Possible, verify with other models
   - Low confidence (<0.50): Likely wrong, look for alternatives

3. **Linguistic Validation**
   - Does the word fit Urdu grammar rules?
   - Does it make semantic sense in context?
   - Is the spelling valid in Urdu orthography?

4. **Context Analysis**
   - Consider surrounding words
   - Maintain sentence coherence
   - Ensure natural Urdu flow

5. **Conflict Resolution**
   - When models disagree, prefer the word that:
     * Has highest confidence AND makes grammatical sense
     * Agrees with at least one other model
     * Fits the semantic context better

**Output:** Only the corrected Urdu sentence with proper punctuation (۔). No explanations.
